Destination Highlights

WHERE IT BEGINS

  • Cần Thơ

    The Mekong capital

    • Floating houses & coconut islets on the delta river
    • Fruit markets, sunrise boat trips & riverside lantern walks

  • Vĩnh Long

    Paddy fields & floating markets

    • Green floodplains, reed forests & slow-paced river towns
    • Rice-paper lanterns, lotus fields & quiet community stays

  • Châu Đốc

    Temples & the dragon-burning festival

    • Five pagodas along the riverside & ancient carvings
    • Long-tail boat rides to caves & the floating markets

  • Phan Thiết

    Coastal capital & deep-sea fishing

    • Long waves along the coast & the famous ocean rowing race
    • Phoenix fruit in season & fishing-boats to hidden reefs

  • Lâm Đồng

    Misty highlands & coffee valleys

    • Fog-wrapped mountains & the flower fields in bloom
    • Highland coffee estates & night markets under the stars
