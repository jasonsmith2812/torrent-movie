About This Tour

Set along the slow meander of the Lower Mekong, Cồn Thới Sơn is a chain of palm-wood islands threaded with coconut-palm stilt homes — the last full day on Vietnam's floating world. This five-day journey steps off the highway and on to the long-tail kayaks, where every meal arrives cooked on your own boat at riverside vendors. Through Cồn Thới Sơn's reed-lined canals you pick river lotus at the farm, mix it into fresh smoothies with the families who harvest it, and watch the islanders press coconut milk into milk-sheets the old way.

Days unwind around sunrise and lantern light rather than clock faces: a morning river swim beside a family's stilt hut, a long-tail boat glide to the floating market where traders still call sale across the water, and an evening return as fireflies rise up from the reeds. By night the group gathers over a slow-cooked riverside grill, shares stories and the taste of the day, then drifts back to a private hammock home. This tour suits small groups and solo travelers who want depth over pace — enough time to slow the river down and meet the people who live its currents.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

SLOW RIVER RITUALS
1
ARRIVAL · CẦN THƠ

Meet, board the floating kitchen

  • Pick-up at Cần Thơ airport or hotel — a greeting with fresh coconut water on arrival.
  • Ease onto a wide long-tail skiff and a riverwalk to the Cồn Thới Sơn stilt-village.
  • Welcome dinner cooked on a riverside grill under bamboo lanterns.
  • Night stay in a private hammock home with a lanai overlooking the canal.
2
RIVER · MARKET & LILY

Kayaking, floating markets and a morning swim

  • Pre-dawn long-tail trip to the Cồn Thới Sơn floating market — browse fruit, catch glimpses of traders at work.
  • River swim beside a homestay's stilt hut; then breakfast of rice-paper noodles by the water.
  • Afternoon kayaking lane through the reed forests, fishing in quiet pockets, and collecting river lotus.
  • Evening lotus smoothie; a slow return as the sun settles over the palm roofs.
3
CULTURE · PAGODA & CAVE

Minh Châu Cave and the riverside pagoda

  • Long-tail boat glide into Tận At Cave — an oyster-shell-walled tunnel carved by boatmen over a century.
  • Visit Pagoda Trang Long and the Long Son Tower — a riverfront landmark of the delta.
  • A riverside family cooking class: from morning-glazed chicken and fish to the shared grilling evening.
  • Free afternoon in a hammock home; sunset river-drift with local tea.
4
GROW · ORCHARD & COCONUT

The coconut farm, orchards and a fruit-harvest day

  • Waking early for a coconut-milk milk-sheet demonstration — a hands-on press by the family.
  • Vineyard and coffee-fruit orchard stroll — a slow tasting of the highland and lowland harvests.
  • Coconut-palm weaving clinic; gather dried fruit and river lotus pressed into the day.
  • Board the long-tail skiff for a farewell candlelit dinner the islanders cook for you.
5
DEPARTURE · SUNRISE

A lantern walk and a slow send-off

  • Rise with the delta for a final sunrise lantern walk cast across the palm canal.
  • A farewell breakfast of river eggs and fruit pressed by the farm family.
  • Checkout and a gentle long-tail transfer back to Cần Thơ airport or your hotel.
  • A keepsake box of dried lotus, a carved mango-wood spoon and this day's itinerary.
