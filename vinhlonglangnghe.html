About This Tour

Tucked south of the Mekong Delta, Vĩnh Long is a slow corner of clay, canals and fire — where the old trade still turns earth into tiles, tiles into pots, and pots into souvenirs passed hand to hand. A whole day on the trail winds through three living workshops: a pagoda the size of a living town, a quiet pottery village where the clay still remembers the hand that shaped it, and the Mang Thít brickyard where the Delta learned to build its own skyline out of red.

This tour suits travelers who want craft over distance — enough of a pace to touch the kilns, watch a pot take shape and leave with something fired the day before. Drive the morning past Vietnam's finest hủ-tiếu, step into Xá Lợi's tile-bright courtyard, pass the pottery village at Tư Buội, and spend your noon before the cracked brick-walls of the great lò gạch — before turning back with dust on your shoes and pottery in your bag.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

CLAY, TILES & CANALS
1
MORNING · PICK-UP & BREAKFAST

Board the SAVACO vehicle — the Vĩnh Long clay trail begins

  • At 06:00, the SAVACO Tourist vehicle and guide meet you at the rendezvous point and set out on the Vĩnh Long one-day tour.
  • On the way stop for breakfast — the local favourite, Mỹ Tho hủ tiếu, served at a riverside eatery.
  • Arrive on the trail of clay pits and old kilns that once powered the Delta's builders.
2
PAGODA · CHÙA PHẬT NGỌC XÁ LỢI

A pagoda the size of a living ancient town

  • Visit Chùa Phật Ngọc Xá Lợi — likened to a miniature cổ trấn, one of Vĩnh Long's most sacred and stately temples.
  • Wander the tile-worn courtyard and the towering main hall where the city still turns to pray.
  • Take a slow turn through the incense-scented aisles before the road to the clay country.
3
TILES · LÒ GẠCH CỔ MANG THÍT

The red-brick heart of the Mekong Delta

  • Stand among the Mang Thít old brickyard — the largest and most famous red-brick and tile maker in the whole Mekong Delta region.
  • Read the kiln walls for the nickname the town still carries — the brick and tile kingdom.
  • Follow the trail of stacked bricks and cooled tiles where the Delta once built its own skyline.
4
NOON · LUNCH & TỪ BỤI POTTERY VILLAGE

Eat, then shape a pot with the village hands

  • Noon lunch at a local restaurant, then head to the Tư Buội pottery village — where the plain craft-spirit of the village still lives.
  • Let the land, water and the hands of the maker turn clay into plain but thoughtful pottery.
  • Try your hand at tô vẽ — painting your own pot before it goes back into the kiln.
5
AFTERNOON · RETURN

Back to Ho Chi Minh City with craft in the bag

  • Drive back to TP. Hồ Chí Minh, closing out a meaningful full day with memories worth keeping.
  • Carry your painted pot, a hủ-tiếu story and the smell of Mang Thít's brickyard.
  • We look forward to meeting you again on your next trip.
